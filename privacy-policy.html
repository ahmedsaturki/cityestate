1. Introduction

Welcome to CityEstate ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our WhatsApp messaging services and real estate platform.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, email address, and property preferences
  • WhatsApp Data: Messages sent and received through our WhatsApp integration
  • Property Information: Properties you inquire about or save
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our services

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your real estate inquiries
  • Send property recommendations matching your preferences
  • Schedule property viewings and appointments
  • Improve our services and customer experience
  • Send updates about new properties (with your consent)

4. WhatsApp Messaging

By providing your phone number and opting in to receive messages, you consent to receiving WhatsApp messages from CityEstate regarding:

  • Property listings and recommendations
  • Appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Market updates and insights

You can opt out of receiving messages at any time by sending "STOP" to our WhatsApp number.

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information. We may share your data with:

  • Property owners (only when you express interest in a specific property)
  • Service providers who assist in our operations
  • Law enforcement when required by law

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
