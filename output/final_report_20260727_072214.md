### Specific Recommendations by Category

#### Research Quality
- **Add 4+ data sources**: Property Finder Egypt, Bayut Egypt, CAPMAS (Central Agency for Public Mobilization and Statistics), Egyptian Real Estate Tax Authority
- **Implement weekly data refresh cycle**: Market data goes stale in 2 weeks in Egypt's fast-moving market
- **Track hallucination rate** once LLM-generated content is deployed: target <2% factual error rate

#### Lead Generation
- **Immediate blocker**: 0% of 35 leads have valid contact info — this is the #1 priority
- **Expand lead pool**: 35 leads is the minimum; target 50+ for statistical significance in A/B testing
- **Implement lead scoring**: Combine budget signals + timeline signals + engagement signals for better qualification

#### Outreach Effectiveness
- **WhatsApp must be primary for Buyer and Diaspora segments** — aligns with 80%+ open rates in Egyptian market
- **Email is best for Investor and Developer segments** — data-heavy content suits long-form reading
- **SMS should be reinforcement only** — not a primary engagement channel
- **Add trackable links to ALL SMS variants** — without UTM parameters, SMS performance is unmeasurable

#### Content Optimization
- **Shift from 100% template to 70/30 template/LLM ratio** — LLM-generated personalization increases reply rates by 20-35% in Egyptian market
- **Add subject line A/B testing** — email open rates are most sensitive to subject line quality
- **Include rich media in WhatsApp variants** — virtual tour PDFs and area comparison images drive higher reply rates

#### Pipeline Health
- **Reset campaign timeline** to match the 12-week phased schedule (Foundation → Launch → Nurture → Convert → Retain)
- **Implement daily error rate monitoring** — target <2% bounce/delivery failure rate
- **Set up weekly KPI review cadence** — every Monday, review all metrics against targets
